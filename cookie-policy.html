Cookie Policy

This policy explains which cookies hyprframe.com uses, what for, how long they last, and how you can accept, decline or change your preferences at any time. HYPRFRAME is the data controller, and the rules that apply here are article 22.2 of Spain's Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) and the GDPR.

What are 'cookies'?

'Cookies' are small text files that are stored by the browser (for example, Chrome or Safari) on your computer or mobile phone. They allow websites to store things like user preferences. You can think of cookies as providing a 'memory' for the website, so that it can recognise you when you come back and respond appropriately.

Alongside cookies we use localStorage, a store in your own browser with the same purpose that is never sent to our server by itself.

How do I turn cookies off?

It is usually possible to stop your browser accepting cookies, or to stop it accepting cookies from a particular website. All modern browsers allow you to change your cookie settings. You can usually find these settings in the 'options' or 'preferences' menu of your browser. To understand these settings, the following links may be helpful, or you can use the 'Help' option in your browser for more details.

  • Cookie settings in Chrome
  • Cookie settings in Firefox
  • Cookie settings in Safari web and iOS
  • Cookie settings in Microsoft Edge
  • Cookie settings in Brave

If you delete the cookieConsent record, we will ask for your consent again on the next visit, as we must.

Types of cookies

Cookies fall into one of two categories — session or persistent — and the type of cookie is determined by how long each cookie is used:

  • Session cookies only last for your online session and disappear from your computer or device when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies stay on your computer or device after the browser has been closed and last for the period of time specified in the cookie. These persistent cookies are activated each time you visit the site where the cookie was generated.

Video Cookies

Always active

We use Vimeo to embed videos on our site. The player is only loaded when you press play on a film, and the embed always uses the dnt=1 setting, which disables Vimeo's own analytics and tracking features. Vimeo still sets the cookies it needs for secure playback and to prevent abuse (like bot traffic); these are considered essential for the video to work properly.

If you visit a page containing a Vimeo video and you are logged in to your Vimeo account, or if you've visited the Vimeo website before our website, Vimeo may use cookies to keep you logged in and may collect data linked to your Vimeo account which we cannot control.

For more details, please see Vimeo's cookie policy.
